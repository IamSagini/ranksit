About Ranksit

Ranksit is an AI content and SEO platform. It turns a target keyword into a researched, publish-ready draft and scores it against a twenty-point SEO audit, so writers, marketers and founders can produce optimised content in minutes instead of hours.

What it does

You enter a keyword, content type and tone. Ranksit searches the web for real, current facts, writes a full article, cites its sources, and grades it across keyword usage, metadata, structure, readability and evidence. A live score shows exactly what to improve, with a specific fix for every check. Finished pieces can be exported as markdown or published to the public Explore feed.

Who builds it

Ranksit is built by Sagini Technologies, a software studio focused on practical AI-powered web products.
