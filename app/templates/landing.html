Frequently asked questions

How does Ranksit generate content?

You enter a target keyword, content type and tone. Ranksit searches the web for real, current facts, writes a full draft, cites its sources as links, and scores it against twenty SEO checks, all in one pass.

Is the content actually researched, or just made up?

It uses live web search to ground each article in real sources and figures, and cites them inline. It is instructed never to invent statistics, and to omit anything it cannot verify.

What does the SEO score measure?

Twenty checks across five areas: keyword usage, metadata, structure, readability and evidence. Each failing check comes with a specific fix, and the score updates live as you edit.

What is the free plan limit?

Ten generations per day on the free plan, which resets daily. The SEO scoring, editing, publishing and export are all unlimited.

Where does my content go after I write it?

Your library is private to you. You can export any piece as markdown to publish on your own site, or publish it to Explore where other users can read and like it.
